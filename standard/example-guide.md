## Live guide

### Opening-question practice

- **Time:** 10 minutes.
- **Purpose:** Practice asking an opening question and receive feedback.
- **Lead:** Facilitator.
- **Materials:** Prompt slide and chat.

**Lead the segment**

1. Introduce the task and display the prompt: "Write one question that would invite everyone into a discussion." Allow 2 minutes.
2. Invite participants to post their questions in chat. Allow 2 minutes.
3. Invite two volunteers to read their questions. Ask the group: "What makes this question easy to respond to?" Allow 4 minutes.
4. Ask participants to revise their questions using the feedback. Allow 2 minutes.

**Live logistics**

- Presenter shares the prompt slide at the start.
- Facilitator pastes the prompt into chat when individual writing begins.
