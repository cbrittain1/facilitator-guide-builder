# Guide requirements

These are starter requirements for the facilitator-guide example. Adapt them to the project and participant outcome during setup. All project paths are relative to the repository root.

Use these requirements with `standard/template.md` and `standard/example-guide.md`. The example shows the expected level of detail; it does not supply facts or activity mechanics for another session.

## What the guide needs

| Requirement | What to check |
|---|---|
| Session context | Identify the session, participant outcome, source files, and draft status. |
| Activity title and purpose | Name each agenda segment and explain its intended participant outcome. |
| Timing | Show segment durations and the session total. Check agreement across the agenda, detailed steps, and source materials. Flag differences without choosing a value silently. |
| Participant instructions | Give actionable steps and include exact prompts when available. A title or "complete the activity" alone is not enough to facilitate an activity. |
| Roles and live logistics | Identify source-supported responsibilities and actions, including handoffs, screen sharing, chat messages, or breakout setup when required. Do not assume a delivery method. |
| Materials and required resource links | Identify materials and include usable links when the activity requires them. A link is not required for every activity. If a required link is missing, flag it instead of inventing one. |
| Preparation | Separate actions needed before the session from actions needed during delivery. |
| Source fidelity | Keep the sequence and relevant details supported by the sources. Identify omissions, disagreements, unclear directions, and draft assumptions. Keep placeholders visibly unfinished. |

If a requirement does not apply, explain why rather than invent content to fill the field. State the reason and evidence when judging a requirement met.

## Review results

- **Meets requirement:** The relevant content is present and supported by the source. This does not mean the whole guide is approved.
- **Missing:** Information needed to satisfy an applicable requirement is absent from the draft or the source.
- **Conflicting:** Relevant source statements or the draft disagree.
- **Needs clarification:** Content exists but is ambiguous or too vague to use.
- **Inferred:** The draft adds a detail not established by the sources. Treat it as a proposed assumption, not a fact.

An unclear requirement should also be referred to the person for clarification. Do not redefine it silently. When more than one result applies, record the distinct issues; do not force them into a single label. For an inapplicable requirement, note the reason instead of marking a missing field as an error.

For unresolved content, use this draft annotation:

> NEEDS CHECKING — [result]: [issue]. Evidence: [file and slide/section]. Confirm: [specific question].

Record results and human resolutions in the short table in `records/review-findings.md`. Keep content results distinct from progress; record a resolution or recheck briefly in the same row, without separate status sections. A person resolves the issue; the changed content is then rechecked against the sources and requirements. Only the person can approve the guide and decide which findings should change instructions or requirements for future work.
