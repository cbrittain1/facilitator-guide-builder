# Check a guide

Objective: determine whether the draft follows the standard, uses the source accurately, and gives facilitators actionable guidance.

1. Compare the draft with the template, source materials, and applicable confirmed decisions.
2. Check for omissions, unsupported additions, timing conflicts, unclear responsibilities, vague actions, and misplaced preparation tasks. Check whether agenda and segment timings agree; leave unresolved values visible.
3. Read relevant human comments and corrections, if available. Preserve unresolved questions and do not assume every suggestion is an approved change.
4. Record each finding in `records/review-findings.md`: finding ID, session and draft version, guide location, issue, source or standard reference, proposed correction, and status.
5. Distinguish a confirmed error from an open question or a proposed design improvement. Keep Lorem Ipsum placeholders visible as unfinished content.
6. Ask for human resolution of open findings. Apply confirmed corrections and record the person's resolution without attributing AI suggestions to them.
7. Recheck affected sections after correction. Do not mark a finding resolved while the guide still contains the issue.
8. Suggest a decision-log entry only when the finding implies a reusable change. Add the confirmed decision and update the relevant instruction when authorized. A finding about one session does not automatically become a rule for every guide.
9. Save a finished guide only after the person approves it. If approval happens with limitations, carry those limitations into the output.
