# Build a guide

Objective: turn session sources into a guide that a facilitator can use during delivery.

1. Read the standard and every relevant source file. Include slide notes, planning notes, and available feedback; do not rely on slide titles alone.
2. Identify participant outcomes, sequence, timing, roles, materials, preparation, and live logistics.
3. Follow the template. Use the example for the level of detail, not as a source of activities or mechanics.
4. Write facilitator actions in sequence, starting with action verbs. Include exact discussion prompts when provided. Name responsibilities when the sources establish them.
5. Put preparation before the session and live actions inside the relevant segment. Include handoffs, chat messages, screen sharing, or breakout instructions only when supported by the sources.
6. Keep source-supported content distinct from inference. Mark inference, missing details, and conflicts as `NEEDS CHECKING`, with a reason and a file/slide reference. Do not silently choose between conflicting sources.
7. Preserve Lorem Ipsum as placeholder content. Do not turn it into invented curriculum or claim that placeholder content is ready for delivery.
8. Save the result as a draft. Identify the source files used. Do not claim human review or final approval.
